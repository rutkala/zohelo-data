Terms of use

Effective 6 September 2026 · Current personal pilot

Purpose and current scope

Zohelo-data is a personal data platform maintained by rutkala. The current version is intended for its owner's data exploration, SQL analysis and research. Availability of the website does not represent a managed service or a promise of continuous operation.

Access and sharing

Use data and connected services only where you have permission. Your Google authorization determines the Drive access granted to the application. A local workspace profile is stored on your device; it is separate from your Google Account.

Review the privacy policy before connecting Drive, using an external AI or database provider, or sharing an analysis. Share only the data, queries and results you intend to disclose.

Source data and results

Source datasets retain their original ownership, licences and attribution requirements. Their inclusion here does not grant additional reuse rights. Check the source provider's terms for your intended use, including commercial use or redistribution.

Data coverage, transformations and automatic updates are still being developed. Check the observation dates, source information and results relevant to your analysis. The portal being available does not establish that its datasets are complete or current.

Software and connected services

The source repository contains the software and its applicable licences. The portal builds on MIT-licensed Duck-UI; dependencies retain their own licences. These usage notes do not replace those licences.

Google, GitHub and any additional provider you choose have their own terms. Configuring another service does not grant a subscription or an entitlement to use its API.

Questions and changes

Contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile or raise a general question in the project repository. The current scope and these pages will be updated as the platform develops.
