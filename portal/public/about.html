Zohelo-data

A workspace for understanding data.

Explore datasets, write SQL and view results in a web interface. Google Drive holds the platform's durable data; DuckDB powers its analytical queries.

Open the data workspace

What the platform does

Zohelo-data brings together data ingestion, stored datasets, transformations and analysis. The current working example explores existing exchange-rate data from the National Bank of Poland (NBP). Completing gold-price processing, governed metrics and the business catalogue is ongoing work.

The current version is a personal pilot for the platform owner. Public information about the application is available here without creating a profile or granting Google access.

Why it connects to Google Drive

When you choose Google sign-in, the portal requests read-only Drive access to find the zohelo-data folders and download selected datasets for analysis. That Google permission covers Drive broadly; the catalogue directs its browsing to the platform folders.

Separate background jobs use the owner's authorization to save ingested data and publish transformed files to Drive. They use their own stored credentials. Signing into the portal does not configure those jobs.

The Google consent screen may identify this project as zohelo-data-ingestor; the web client is named zohelo-data-portal.

Your data and your choices

Local queries run in your browser. Profiles, SQL history and other workspace settings can stay on your device. Optional remote AI, database connections and sharing features can send information outside the browser when used.

Read the privacy policy for access, storage, sharing and deletion details, and the terms of use for the current pilot.

Project and contact

The project is maintained by rutkala on GitHub. The source repository contains the implementation and development plans. The web workspace is based on Duck-UI, with its original attribution and licence retained.
