# Poland governance, economy, registers and public-finance sources

Research date: 2026-09-08. This inventory contains 34 named products. It excludes GUS, BDL, DBW, TERYT, REGON, ZUS, health, education, agriculture, environmental, geospatial and transport sources assigned elsewhere. It also excludes commercial market-data vendors. No endpoint was connected to, no account was created, and no data was harvested. Statements below describe documentation or catalogue pages; all ingestion language is a design proposal.

## Domain synthesis

Poland has strong public first-party coverage across six connected layers. Business identity is split between KRS for court-registered entities and CEIDG for sole traders and other natural-person business activity. RDF adds filed financial documents for KRS entities, while the VAT list adds dated tax status and business settlement accounts. CRBR adds beneficial-owner and control relationships, but it also creates the highest personal-data and permitted-use risk in this group. These products should be linked, not flattened: KRS number, NIP and REGON can be crosswalk candidates, yet each register has its own scope, effective dates and legal meaning.

Public finance is unusually useful at both national and local levels. Ministry of Finance products cover state-budget plans and execution, Treasury debt, public-finance-sector debt, and individual local-government reporting lines. The local-government files are the best candidate here for fine-grained fiscal analytics, because the official page describes databases of individual JST reports as well as aggregates, annual execution information and multiannual financial forecasts, with year sections from 2018 and an archive reaching 2001 ([Ministry of Finance local-government budget reports](https://www.gov.pl/web/finanse/sprawozdania-budzetowe)). Treasury debt and public-sector debt must remain separate: they use different institutional scopes and consolidation concepts. They cannot be added to produce a broader debt figure.

The legislative chain is also unusually linkable. RCL publishes government drafts, impact assessments and consultation files; the Sejm API covers members, committees, prints, votes and parliamentary questions; Senate pages cover the upper-chamber stage; ELI provides durable identifiers and representations for promulgated acts. ISAP remains valuable as an archive and alternative presentation, but it overlaps substantially with ELI. A future model should store events and documents at each stage and use explicit relationship tables between RCL project IDs, Sejm/Senate print identifiers and ELI journal positions. Title matching alone is too ambiguous.

Procurement has two first-party channels. The national e-Zamówienia platform documents a read API for BZP notices and notice statistics. The official integration page says this read access does not require the integration procedure that applies to other platform APIs ([e-Zamówienia integration materials](https://ezamowienia.gov.pl/pl/integracja/)). TED provides anonymous API access for searching and retrieving published EU procurement notices, while unpublished-notice services require an API key ([TED API 3.0](https://docs.ted.europa.eu/api/latest/index.html)). BZP and TED overlap for some procurements but do not share identical publication scope. A notice is not necessarily a procedure, lot, award or signed contract, so those entities must be modeled separately.

NBP supplies a dependable existing baseline for exchange rates and gold, plus separate external, monetary, banking and interest-rate statistics. The FX/gold API is the most precisely documented product in this slice: FX history starts on 2 January 2002, gold on 2 January 2013, and a single request cannot span more than 93 days ([NBP Web API](https://api.nbp.pl/en.html)). The other NBP statistical families are richer but primarily file/publication oriented and need table-by-table metadata review. Release calendars and page update dates should not be mistaken for observation-level change feeds.

Regulatory and enforcement products require more semantic caution than ordinary statistics. KNF’s supervised-entity pages span banks, capital-market firms, insurers, pension entities, payment institutions and other categories ([KNF supervised entities](https://www.knf.gov.pl/podmioty)). Its warning list records notifications of suspected offences and procedural information, not final determinations of liability ([KNF Public Warning List](https://www.knf.gov.pl/dla_konsumenta/ostrzezenia_publiczne)). UOKiK decisions likewise need separate fields for decision, appeal and finality. NIK audit results are documents and findings, not a time series, even where they contain numeric evidence.

Justice and public-safety coverage is mainly downloadable publications rather than APIs. The Ministry of Justice statistical database separates annual and multi-year outputs ([ISWS statistical database](https://isws.ms.gov.pl/pl/baza-statystyczna/)); the Prison Service publishes monthly, quarterly and annual statistics; and Police Headquarters organizes statistics by offence and selected topics ([Police Statistics portal](https://statystyka.policja.pl/)). These sources describe different stages and populations. A police-recorded offence, prosecuted case, court case and imprisoned person must never be treated as the same event or denominator.

## Strongest source dossiers and deeper caveats

### 1. KRS API, RDF and CEIDG form a complementary business graph

The official KRS documentation describes a REST API whose information scope corresponds to KRS extracts ([Open KRS API](https://prs.ms.gov.pl/krs/openApi)). This makes KRS the strongest programmable legal-entity source in this inventory. The proposed canonical keys are KRS number for the entity and explicit identifiers for register sections, roles and extract type. NIP and REGON should be stored as asserted crosswalks rather than assumed unique global keys. “Current” and “full” extracts should remain distinct artifacts because their semantic purpose differs.

RDF is a separate source product, not a KRS API endpoint family. Its viewer instructs users to search by KRS number and download filed documents ([RDF viewer](https://rdf-przegladarka.ms.gov.pl/)). Financial statements can be signed structured documents, but the public viewer is a lookup interface; no official bulk API was found. Free viewing does not establish permission for automated enumeration, bulk retrieval or republication. The appropriate first step is a legal and technical review of document rights and interface conditions. If later approved, original signed documents should be retained separately from parsed facts, with taxonomy version, reporting period, units and sign conventions.

The CEIDG/Biznes.gov.pl warehouse covers a different entity universe centered on entrepreneurs and business activities. The official warehouse page advertises data, reports and statistics about firms registered in CEIDG ([CEIDG and Biznes.gov.pl Data Warehouse](https://dane.biznes.gov.pl/)); official legal notes describe it as the data source for the warehouse ([legal notes](https://www.biznes.gov.pl/en/portal/034100)). Integrator documentation must be reviewed for current authentication, quotas, fields and change filters. Because CEIDG concerns natural persons, the silver model should separate the entrepreneur from activities and establishments and should omit contact or personal fields unless they are necessary for an approved use.

### 2. CRBR should remain a lookup-only candidate until rights and purpose are resolved

CRBR publishes entity-to-beneficial-owner and control information through an official register interface ([CRBR portal](https://www.podatki.gov.pl/crbr/)). It is analytically valuable for ownership and compliance use cases, but its records identify natural persons and describe control relationships. No public bulk or API interface was documented in the pages reviewed. The visible fact that a record is searchable is not evidence that building a commercial mirror, profiling people or retaining all historical responses is permitted.

The proposed priority is therefore conditional. Zohelo-data should first define a specific purpose, minimum necessary fields, retention period, correction handling and access-control policy. Until an authorized machine interface and reuse basis are established, product design should expose an official verification link rather than ingesting CRBR. KRS representation and shareholder data cannot substitute for CRBR because the registers encode different concepts of ownership, representation and beneficial control.

### 3. The Ministry of Finance JST reports are the best fine-grained fiscal source

The local-government budget-report page says the publication includes databases from individual reports, aggregate statements for municipalities, counties, cities with county rights, voivodeships and JST associations, annual budget-execution information and multiannual forecasts. That combination supports both detailed facts and official comparison totals. A proposed silver grain is reporting unit × report form × reporting period × budget classification × measure. TERYT or REGON may be useful keys where present, but identifier availability needs file inspection.

The reporting forms are not interchangeable. Revenue, expenditure, liabilities, receivables, cash and multiannual forecasts express different accounting concepts. Published aggregates may contain the same underlying submissions as unit-level files, so summing both would double count. Monthly and quarterly forms can also overlap with annual totals. Corrections may replace earlier files without a row-level change log; a loader should retain file vintage and reprocess affected periods. The page’s publication or modification timestamp is evidence of portal activity, not evidence that each row changed.

State-budget execution, Treasury debt and public-finance-sector debt deserve separate products for the same reason. The state-budget page links budget acts, execution, classifications and related fiscal material and explains the annual budget process ([State budget](https://www.gov.pl/web/finanse/budzet-panstwa)). The debt products differ by perimeter and methodology. A gold layer should publish each official concept with its own label rather than invent a blended “Polish debt” total.

### 4. e-Zamówienia BZP and TED can form a versioned procurement model

The e-Zamówienia integration page lists APIs for reading BZP notices/statistics, authentication, submitting notices, publishing procurement plans, annual reporting, OCID creation and OCDS event transfer. It explicitly says that reading BZP notices and statistics does not require the integration procedure; other APIs do. For this research scope, only the public read API is a review-first intake candidate. Submission and integration APIs are operational services, not data sources for an initial warehouse.

The BZP model needs notice versions. Procurement plans, contract notices, changes, award/result notices and annual reports can all refer to related activity. Contracting-authority identifiers, notice IDs, OCIDs, procedure IDs and lot IDs should be preserved when present. Amounts need currency and value-type fields; a maximum value, estimated value and awarded value are not the same measure.

TED’s documentation is stronger on authentication boundaries. Anonymous callers may search and retrieve published notices, while services that manipulate unpublished notices require keys. TED API v3 uses a unified gateway and keeps v2 active during transition. The documentation also notes that eForms applications entered production on 14 November 2022. Historical intake must therefore partition legacy XML and eForms schemas. Poland relevance should be derived from documented buyer/place/country fields, not from Polish-language text. BZP and TED records should be related by official identifiers where possible and otherwise treated as possibly overlapping, never blindly deduplicated.

### 5. Sejm API and ELI provide the best programmable governance spine

The Sejm API documentation lists MPs, clubs, committees, votes, prints, interpellations, parliamentary questions and media resources ([Sejm API](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm.html)). The natural key always includes the parliamentary term. Memberships and club affiliations are effective-dated; vote facts belong to a sitting and vote number; prints and interpellations are documents with their own IDs. A single wide “parliamentary event” table would blur these distinctions.

ELI provides a stable URI template using journal, year, position, text type, language and representation format. Its official description states that years run from 1918 to the current year and distinguishes published text (`ogl`), consolidated text (`tj`) and codified text (`uj`). The page warns through its examples that a codified text may lack legal force ([Polish ELI](https://eli.gov.pl/)). That status distinction belongs in the data model and user interface.

RCL is the upstream government-draft layer. Its search page exposes project type, title, creation date, applicant, work-program number and process flags, and groups projects into in-progress, adopted and archived sets ([Government Legislative Process portal](https://legislacja.rcl.gov.pl/)). Public consultation submissions and impact assessments may contain names and free text, so attachment handling needs data-minimisation and rights review. No bulk API was verified. ISAP should be retained as a specialist archive/fallback, while ELI becomes the preferred official-act identifier.

### 6. NBP products require table-specific contracts outside the FX API

The FX/gold API can be onboarded with bounded date windows and explicit table semantics. Table A and B publish mid rates for different currency sets; table C publishes buy/sell rates. Gold is priced per gram and has its own history. A full load can be partitioned into 93-day or smaller windows, while incremental polling can use effective/publication dates. Neither method is source CDC, and unavailable dates should not be interpreted as zero observations.

Balance of payments, international investment position, monetary aggregates, bank/MFI balance sheets and interest rates are separate statistical families. Flow versus stock is the central modeling divide. BOP records period transactions; the international investment position records stocks that can change from transactions, prices, exchange rates and other adjustments. Monetary aggregates contain hierarchical totals and counterparts. Bank balance sheets may publish stocks, transactions and growth rates. Interest-rate percentages are non-additive and differ between policy rates, new business and outstanding business. These products should use separate contracts and preserve official metadata, frequency, unit and vintage.

### 7. Warning, decision and audit products need status-aware use

KNF’s warning list includes named subjects, available KRS/NIP/REGON identifiers, prosecutors, legal provisions and procedural notes. The official page states that it contains KNF notifications of suspected offences and certain criminal proceedings. Absence from the list is expressly not a substitute for due diligence. A future model must preserve allegation language, reporting authority, procedural stage and update date and must never turn inclusion into a generic “illegal company” flag.

UOKiK decision records and concentration cases need similar status handling. A merger-control decision does not prove that a transaction completed, and a competition or consumer decision may be appealed. Case number, parties, document date, decision outcome, conditions and appeal/finality information should be separate fields. Concentration records may also be a subset or alternate view of the broader decision database, so the same case should not create two independent enforcement facts.

NIK’s search interface supports publication periods, audit divisions, conducting units, audited units, audit numbers and document types ([NIK audit results](https://www.nik.gov.pl/kontrole/wyniki-kontroli-nik/)). These are primary audit documents. Any numeric findings extracted from them need document-page citations and the audited period; they should not enter a general statistics fact table without provenance.

## Missing coverage and unresolved questions

The main gap is reusable, documented bulk access for person-linked registers. CRBR exposes public lookup but no verified bulk/API route. RDF also offers document discovery and download without a documented bulk API. CEIDG appears to offer programmatic access, but current authentication, quotas, historical change retention and permitted commercial reuse need confirmation from the latest integrator terms. KRS API access is clear, yet enumeration rules, rate limits and redistribution terms remain unresolved.

Licensing is the most consistent cross-source uncertainty. Gov.pl states CC BY-SA 4.0 terms for site text, but that does not automatically settle every attached spreadsheet, database or third-party document. For NBP, KNF, UOKiK, Parliament, PKW, justice, prison and police products, access is usually clear while commercial redistribution terms are not. Each onboarding decision needs a file- or service-specific rights check. The dane.gov.pl catalogue helps discover licence metadata, but it is a gateway: its catalogue record cannot override the upstream publisher’s terms or turn a mirrored resource into a new observation source.

Machine access is uneven. Official sources advertise API routes for Sejm, ELI, KRS, BZP, TED, NBP FX/gold and dane.gov.pl; the inventory distinguishes opened access documentation from catalogue-only evidence. Senate, RCL, NIK, UOKiK, Police and much of the justice data are primarily HTML/document collections. No scraping bypass should be designed where a source provides only human browsing. A source can still be useful as a linked official evidence product without being ingested.

History and revisions also need direct file inspection. Many pages group releases by year but do not publish a universal first observation date or correction protocol. Update dates on websites indicate publication activity; they are not equivalent to record effective dates, reporting periods or change-data capture. Every production proposal should preserve source vintage, use explicit observation periods and recheck recent history where corrections are possible.

Finally, several semantic bridges remain manual design work: KRS/NIP/REGON matching across business registers; JST identifiers across changing administrative structures; RCL-to-Sejm-to-Senate-to-ELI legislative links; and BZP-to-TED procurement links. These bridges should record match method and confidence. They should never silently merge records based only on names.
