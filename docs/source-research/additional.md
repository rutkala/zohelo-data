# Additional coverage: innovation and emergency services

The Patent Office of Poland exposes an official index of statistical APIs covering
applications and grants by origin, applicant type and voivodeship. This is a promising
aggregate-data complement to economic and business statistics, subject to checking the
individual endpoint formats, period coverage and terms. Its e-Wyszukiwarka is a separate
record-search product; public search does not establish an authorized bulk backfile.
[UPRP statistical APIs](https://uprp.gov.pl/pl/o-urzedzie/statystyki/api-do-statystyk),
[UPRP search](https://uprp.gov.pl/pl/uslugi-online/e-wyszukiwarka).

EPO OPS offers registered programmatic access with a documented free volume threshold.
Its terms distinguish incorporating data in products from distributing the data as such.
Consequently it belongs in conditional review for a downstream product, rather than an
unrestricted open-data export layer. A Polish application, an international publication and
a patent family cannot be treated as interchangeable counts.
[OPS](https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/data/web-services/ops),
[OPS terms](https://www.epo.org/en/service-support/ordering/terms-and-conditions/ops-terms-and-conditions).

KG PSP provides annual intervention archives and a current casualty summary that states
daily updating. Those interfaces need different handling: historical file inventory and
revision comparison for the archive, and carefully labelled snapshots for current
aggregates. Confirm each metric's reporting period before deriving daily incident counts.
The pages request source attribution and point to the national catalogue for licence
information. [Intervention archive](https://www.gov.pl/web/kgpsp/interwencje-psp),
[current summaries](https://www.gov.pl/web/kgpsp/ofiary-polska).

The [official ministry and subordinate-body directory](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/ministerstwa)
is a discovery route for the Polish long tail. It identifies organizations, not an inventory
of all their datasets or an access licence. Extend coverage through named publication
channels and agency catalogues, recording reorganizations and predecessor organizations.
This avoids losing historical series when a ministry or publisher changes its name.
