# Poland environment, geospatial, energy, infrastructure and local data

Research date: 2026-09-08. This review covers 35 named products or coherent product families from official Polish publishers. It is source research and architecture guidance only: no endpoint was connected, no payload was harvested, no account was created and no authentication flow was attempted. “Public” and “free” describe access evidenced on an official page; they do not automatically mean that bulk redistribution or commercial reuse is permitted.

## Domain synthesis

Poland has an unusually strong public geospatial spine, but its access modes have to be read literally. GUGiK publishes administrative geography, geographical names, topography, imagery and elevation as downloadable resources or feature services. The cadastral and address layers are different: the national EGiB service federates county data, while the documented national address integration is a viewing service. A WMS can locate or render data without providing reusable objects. This distinction is the central implementation risk in the Polish landscape.

Environmental observations split cleanly by function. IMGW-PIB supplies operational meteorological and hydrological observations and a separate file archive. GIOŚ publishes air-quality measurements and water-quality monitoring, while Wody Polskie supplies water-management and flood/drought planning geography. PIG-PIB adds geology, mineral deposits and hydrogeological maps. GDOŚ covers protected areas, species and habitats; the forestry Bank Danych o Lasach adds forest management geometry. These products overlap spatially but measure or designate different concepts.

Energy and infrastructure coverage is broad but less uniformly reusable. PSE exposes operational electricity and balancing-market reports through its current API v2. URE publishes regulatory indicators and renewable-installation aggregates. GAZ-SYSTEM publishes transmission transparency data while reserving shipper-specific information for its authenticated system. GDDKiA provides periodic road counts and project/infrastructure downloads. CEPiK has a documented public API and explicit reuse statement. UTK and ULC publish rail and aviation statistics, respectively. Port Gdańsk supplies useful port-specific releases, but this review did not find a national, structured maritime series with comparable port coverage.

The five city products show why municipal transport must be modeled per producer. Gdańsk, Poznań and Kraków publish explicit GTFS-Realtime resources. Wrocław documents CSV/JSON vehicle positions tied to GTFS, while Warsaw is migrating its open-data portal and the exact current transport contracts need revalidation. Realtime feeds are snapshots, not historical event logs; an archive must not be inferred from their existence.

The review also includes two infrastructure gaps requested for explicit coverage. GUNB’s RWDZ exposes construction applications, decisions and notifications through public search and documented API material. It is a permit process register, not a completed-building inventory. internet.gov.pl provides address-level broadband availability/planning context, while UKE publishes market aggregates; these grains should remain separate.

## Strongest source dossiers

### 1. GUGiK national geospatial products

**Official facts.** GUGiK’s free-download notice names PRG, PRNG, BDOO, orthoimagery, elevation products and BDOT10k among resources made available without a request ([GUGiK free-download notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/gugik/dane-udostepniane-bez-platnie-do-pobrania-z-serwisu-wwwgeoportalgovpl)). BDOT10k is available in packages by county, voivodeship or country. Its page explains that a WMS `GetFeatureInfo` response can expose package links; the downloaded object package is the reusable data, while the WMS is the locator/presentation layer ([BDOT10k](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/pl/dane/baza-danych-obiektow-topograficznych-bdot10k/)).

Orthoimagery makes the same boundary visible in another way. GUGiK offers raster download and WMS/WMTS viewing, plus WFS footprint/index services by acquisition year. The index is useful for asset discovery, but it is not the raster itself. The official index spans years from 1957 through 2026, although raw-image availability and resolution still vary by footprint ([Ortofotomapa](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/pl/dane/ortofotomapa-orto/)). NMT/NMPT/LiDAR products likewise require retention of acquisition epoch, horizontal CRS and vertical datum; the NMT examples include EPSG:2180 ([NMT](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/pl/dane/numeryczny-model-terenu-nmt/)).

The strongest caution concerns EGiB. GUGiK describes a 2025 national aggregation from 385 county WFS services with daily pulls. Parcel and building coverage is limited to the data each county actually exposes ([EGiB](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/pl/dane/ewidencja-gruntow-i-budynkow-egib/)). The address-integration notice documents a national WMS, not an address-object download ([address service notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/gugik/nowy-adres-uslug-dotyczacych-numeracji-porzadkowej)). GESUT sharpens the contrast: the national utilities integration is a WMS view, while vector data are obtained from county authorities and may be paid ([GESUT](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/pl/dane/uzbrojenie-terenu-gesut/)).

**Design recommendations.** Use PRG as the versioned geographic backbone and preserve native IDs, edition and effective dates. Ingest BDOT10k from object packages, not rendered services. Treat ortho and elevation indexes as catalogues pointing to immutable assets. Snapshot EGiB per county and load date, preserving the contributing service on every feature. Keep a coverage matrix so a missing parcel or building is not interpreted as real-world absence. Do not onboard the national address or GESUT view until an authorized feature/bulk source and its reuse terms are established.

### 2. IMGW live API and historical archive

**Official facts.** IMGW’s public-data page separates live API endpoints for synoptic, hydrological and meteorological observations and warnings from archive file resources. It advertises JSON, XML, CSV and HTML representations. The same page states conditions for reuse, including source attribution and the possibility of commercial reuse ([IMGW API information and terms](https://danepubliczne.imgw.pl/apiinfo)). The hydrological archive is organized into period directories such as daily, monthly and longer aggregates, with station lists and downloadable files ([hydrological archive](https://danepubliczne.imgw.pl/data/dane_pomiarowo_obserwacyjne/dane_hydrologiczne/)). Neither page establishes a single retention window, a numeric request quota or a uniform release lag for every series.

**Design recommendations.** Build two connectors. Use the file archive for the historical baseline, recording path, file modification time, checksum, station dictionary version and observation period. Use the live API for current observations and warnings. Reconcile their overlap by station, parameter and observation time, but retain both source versions until revision behavior is understood. Store the original time text and offset; normalize analytics to UTC while retaining `Europe/Warsaw` civil-time semantics and DST transitions. Keep quality/status codes and separate observation time from publication or download time. Confirm the exact attribution wording during legal review rather than paraphrasing it in exported products.

### 3. GIOŚ and Wody Polskie water/air products

**Official facts.** The GIOŚ air API publishes station/sensor readings and indexes through JSON-LD/OpenAPI. GIOŚ requires attribution and warns that current readings are unverified, can change, and can be interrupted by telemetry failures. Its API page also states that all pollutant values are delivered in µg/m³, including CO, which differs from some human-facing conventions ([GIOŚ Air Quality API](https://powietrze.gios.gov.pl/pjp/content/api)).

GIOŚ’s water-monitoring publication distinguishes provisional current-year data from subsequently verified results; the 2026 page says verified data are available after the stated following-year deadline ([surface-water publication](https://wody.gios.gov.pl/pjwp/publication/367)). Groundwater results are a separate stream linked to PIG’s monitoring network and annual files. Wody Polskie, by contrast, publishes water-management geography. Its official SIGW access page lists flood-hazard/risk, water-body and planning themes, describes free Hydroportal viewing, and says discovery, view and download services are supplied where feasible ([SIGW data provision](https://www.gov.pl/web/wody-polskie/udostepnianie-danych-z-systemu-informacyjnego-gospodarowania-wodami)).

**Design recommendations.** Model GIOŚ observations at station/sensor/parameter/time grain with a provisional/verified status and publication version. Never replace provisional measurements without retaining their lineage. Keep sample results and water-body assessments as separate facts. Model Wody Polskie maps by plan edition, scenario and probability; a flood-hazard zone is neither a measured river level nor a water-quality sample. Inventory each SIGW theme separately because download format, history, fee and reuse conditions may differ. Confirm GIOŚ API protection limits in the OpenAPI document before scheduling any polling.

### 4. Geology, protected nature and forests

**Official facts.** PIG-PIB’s Central Geological Database describes the borehole subsystem as holding records from the late nineteenth century to the present and offers maps, thematic services and SHP download ([CBDG boreholes](https://baza.pgi.gov.pl/podsystemy/otwory)). MIDAS is PIG’s principal database for mineral deposits, mining areas/lands and concessions ([MIDAS](https://www.pgi.gov.pl/midas.html)). Hydrogeological map access is mixed: JPG/PDF maps are public, while database files in MDB/SHP are supplied on request; documented coordinate systems include PL-1992 and legacy PL-1942 depending on the layer ([MHP data access](https://www.pgi.gov.pl/psh/dane-hydrogeologiczne-psh/947-bazy-danych-hydrogeologiczne/8888-dane-hydrogeologiczne-mhp.html)).

GDOŚ publishes WMS and WFS for the Central Register of Nature Protection Forms and protected-species/habitat resources. It warns that CRFOP updates depend on regional and local authorities and that legal acts, rather than the displayed geometry alone, determine protected boundaries ([GDOŚ geospatial access](https://www.gov.pl/web/gdos/dostep-do-danych-geoprzestrzennych)). Lasy Państwowe’s Bank Danych o Lasach documents WMS/WMTS, WFS and OGC API Features across forest, tourism and fire-road themes ([forest BDL OGC services](https://www9.bdl.lasy.gov.pl/portal/uslugi-ogc?v=1)). This BDL is the forest database, distinct from GUS Bank Danych Lokalnych.

**Design recommendations.** Onboard named PIG subsystems rather than treating CBDG as one schema. Store borehole positional quality and source era. Split MIDAS deposits, legal mining areas and concessions, preserving status and validity. Keep MHP request-gated vectors conditional until access, costs and reuse are resolved. Link GDOŚ features to the governing legal act and show a boundary-quality warning in downstream products. Inventory forest layers by interface because WMS-only themes cannot be promoted to features.

### 5. Electricity, regulatory energy and gas transparency

**Official facts.** PSE migrated its public reports service to API v2 in 2025 and announced retirement of v1 at the end of that year. The service covers power-system and balancing-market reporting for market participants, researchers and public administration ([PSE API v2 transition](https://www.pse.pl/-/najwazniejsze-dane-o-pracy-systemu-elektroenergetycznego-i-rynku-bilansujacego-w-nowej-odslonie); [current API](https://api.raporty.pse.pl/)). URE publishes price indicators on quarterly and annual schedules and has annual small-renewable-installation reporting since 2016; its description notes a scope definition change from 2021 ([price indicators](https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/energia-elektryczna/ceny-wskazniki); [small renewable installations](https://www.ure.gov.pl/pl/urzad/informacje-ogolne/aktualnosci/12642,Male-instalacje-OZE-w-2024-r.html)). GAZ-SYSTEM’s transparency page links public capacity, nominations, actual flows, calorific value and planned-work information, while shipper-specific imbalance information is accessed through the authenticated SWI system ([GAZ-SYSTEM transparency](https://www.gaz-system.pl/en/for-customers/market-information/data-transparency.html)).

**Design recommendations.** Start with a PSE endpoint inventory and treat report code, business date, interval and published revision as the natural grain. Verify whether timestamps use UTC or Polish market time before transformation. Retain PSE report versions rather than assuming append-only behavior. Model URE indicators with their definitions and validity periods; do not splice installation aggregates across the 2021 definition boundary without a break flag. For gas, preserve point code, direction, gas day and unit. Restrict initial GAZ-SYSTEM scope to clearly public transparency products; authenticated shipper information is a separate access class.

### 6. Transport, construction and broadband infrastructure

**Official facts.** GDDKiA’s 2020/2021 General Traffic Measurement covers measured sections of national and voivodeship roads and is a periodic campaign rather than a live traffic feed ([GPR 2020/2021](https://www.gov.pl/web/gddkia/generalny-pomiar-ruchu-20202021)). Its download page separately publishes road-programme XML/XSD and service-area/parking lists ([GDDKiA downloads](https://www.gov.pl/web/gddkia/dane-do-pobrania)). CEPiK’s official API page says selected vehicle data are free to use commercially or noncommercially with source attribution and describes more than 60 vehicle parameters ([CEPiK API](https://www.gov.pl/web/cepik/api-dla-centralnej-ewidencji-pojazdow-i-kierowcow-api-do-cepik)). UTK’s rail portal covers passenger, freight, punctuality, station/regional and rolling-stock statistics ([Dane UTK](https://dane.utk.gov.pl/)); ULC publishes quarterly airport tables extending back at least to 2008–2010 for some measures ([ULC airport statistics](https://ulc.gov.pl/statystyki-analizy/statystyki-wg-portow-lotniczych)).

GUNB’s public RWDZ search covers construction-permit applications, decisions and notifications, and an official OpenAPI documentation surface is available ([RWDZ search](https://wyszukiwarka.gunb.gov.pl/); [RWDZ API docs](https://dev.wyszukiwarka.gunb.gov.pl/docs)). internet.gov.pl supports address-level checks of internet availability and planned investments ([official guide](https://www.gov.pl/web/5g/sprawdz-dostepnosc-internetu-w-swojej-okolicy-przewodnik-po-internetgovpl)); UKE’s annual report supplies market-level infrastructure and sector aggregates ([UKE 2025 report](https://www.uke.gov.pl/akt/raport-o-stanie-rynku-komunikacji-elektronicznej-w-2025-roku,670.html)).

**Design recommendations.** Version GPR road sections against their measurement-edition network; section changes make naive year-to-year joins unsafe. Treat RWDZ records as administrative events and never equate permit approval with a finished building. Resolve parcel/address text probabilistically and retain the original. Keep internet.gov.pl provider coverage claims, planned investment and user demand as different fact types with report vintage. Do not aggregate them with UKE market totals. The public broadband bulk/API route, stable address key, history and reuse license remain onboarding blockers.

### 7. Municipal portals and realtime transport

**Official facts.** Gdańsk links its municipal open-data API and transit resources; its TRISTAR catalogue explicitly identifies GTFS-Realtime trip updates and vehicle positions, while the static GTFS resource says its horizon is no more than the next 14 days ([Gdańsk Open Data](https://www.gdansk.pl/otwarte-dane-w-gdansku); [TRISTAR GTFS-RT](https://ckan.multimediagdansk.pl/dataset/tristar/resource/976e1fd1-73d9-4237-b6ba-3c06004d1105); [static GTFS](https://ckan.multimediagdansk.pl/dataset/tristar/resource/30e783e4-2bec-4a7d-bb22-ee3e3b26ca96)). Poznań publishes direct protobuf files for trip updates and vehicle positions ([Poznań GTFS-RT](https://www.ztm.poznan.pl/otwarte-dane/gtfs-rt/)). Kraków exposes static GTFS ZIPs and realtime alerts, trip updates and vehicle positions through the ZTP feed index ([Kraków feeds](https://gtfs.ztp.krakow.pl/)). Wrocław’s vehicle metadata documents CSV/JSON positions refreshed every ten minutes and join fields for its GTFS schedule ([Wrocław vehicle positions](https://open-data.cui.wroclaw.pl/hdb/metadane/51/)). Warsaw says its new portal is replacing `api.um.warszawa.pl`, so older registration and endpoint assumptions need rechecking ([Warsaw open-data transition](https://warszawa19115.pl/-/dane-po-warszawsku)).

**Design recommendations.** Archive each static GTFS package with download time and hash only if local terms permit. GTFS identifiers are feed-local, so namespace them by publisher and feed version. Interpret service dates and times above 24:00 under `Europe/Warsaw`; preserve realtime header/entity timestamps and UTC offsets. Treat realtime protobuf/CSV files as current-state snapshots unless an official historical service is documented. Record service-area limits separately from municipal boundaries. A city portal may catalogue the producer feed; ingest the producer payload once and retain the portal record as discovery lineage.

### 8. Rejestr Urbanistyczny transition

**Official facts.** The Rejestr Urbanistyczny launched on 1 July 2026. Official transition guidance says BIP publication continued through September for relevant materials, with central publication expanding from October; offices have until 30 November 2026 to populate current spatial data ([register information](https://www.gov.pl/web/zagospodarowanieprzestrzenne/rejestr-urbanistyczny-informacje); [launch notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/zagospodarowanieprzestrzenne/rejestr-urbanistyczny-uruchomiony)). Geoportal documents free WMS layers for planning-document categories and a data list, updated by local authorities ([Geoportal services](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/aktualnosci/nowe-uslugi-w-geoportalu-rejestr-urbanistyczny/)). As of the research date, the register is live but still within the statutory population window.

**Design recommendations.** Keep this source conditional until after 30 November 2026. Recheck national completeness, authority coverage, identifiers and whether feature/download services exist beyond WMS and linked lists. Model the legal document, its spatial representation, adoption/effective dates, status and supersession separately. Preserve prior BIP URLs during transition. A map depiction should not be treated as a parcel-level legal fact without its underlying document.

## Missing coverage and long-tail discovery

Several useful areas remain outside initial onboarding. GESUT vector utility networks are county-supplied and potentially paid; the national integration is view-only. BDO waste reporting was not included because this review did not verify a stable public aggregate product, machine-readable history and commercial reuse terms. National maritime coverage remains incomplete: the inventory contains a Port Gdańsk release product, not a comparable all-port database. Municipal parking, cycling, environmental sensors, waste collection, local planning and roadworks are fragmented across hundreds of authorities. Local data may also be mirrored on dane.gov.pl, which is useful for discovery but does not replace the producer’s terms or provenance.

Use a repeatable municipality discovery method:

1. Start from the municipality’s official BIP and open-data domain, then search its CKAN/API catalogue for transport, environment, utilities, buildings, planning and mobility datasets.
2. Cross-check dane.gov.pl by publisher, but follow every candidate to the municipal producer page. Record the producer, dataset ID, territorial scope and last update; treat the national catalogue as lineage, not a second observation source.
3. Inspect the interface literally. Classify HTML/PDF, WMS/WMTS, WFS/OGC API Features, static bulk files, GTFS and GTFS-Realtime separately. Require a feature or bulk route for object ingestion; never infer one from a viewer.
4. Capture the local terms page and dataset-level notice. Record commercial reuse, attribution, personal-data constraints and whether free access is only for viewing. Unknown remains a blocker.
5. Profile metadata without connecting to data: expected format, documented identifiers, CRS, timezone, refresh statement, geographic boundary, historical depth and archive links. After approval, test one low-impact request under the stated limits.
6. Namespace local IDs by producer and feed version. Crosswalk to versioned PRG geography and retain native labels. For GTFS, keep agency/service-area boundaries; for geospatial assets, preserve source CRS and survey/edition date.
7. Rank portals by object-level access, licensing clarity, history, stable identifiers and update evidence. Revisit transitional or viewer-only sources rather than scraping them.

The practical first review queue is PRG, BDOT10k, EGiB, IMGW live/archive, GIOŚ air and surface water, Wody Polskie, PSE, CEPiK, GUNB RWDZ, the forest BDL and the five municipal mobility products. Their official evidence is comparatively strong, but licensing, numeric quotas, revision semantics and historical completeness still need source-by-source verification before connection.
