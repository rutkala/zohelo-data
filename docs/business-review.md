# Business review and decisions

The single current record of project status and open owner questions is [the delivery plan](deliverables.md). The data portal and catalogue do not host project-management or owner-review flows.

Answer a listed question in chat with its ID and your choice or context. The assistant will record the answer in the delivery plan and use the linked research only as input to the next technical step.

## Recovery

If you lose a draft or cannot find the question, reopen [the delivery plan](deliverables.md), copy the relevant question ID, and send the ID with your answer in chat. If the repository page is unavailable, share the answer and any available context in chat; the assistant will restore it to the canonical record. No browser-local response is required or treated as approval.
